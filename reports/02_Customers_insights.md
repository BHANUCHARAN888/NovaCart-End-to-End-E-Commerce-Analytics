## Customer Insight 1

### Business Question
Which customer segments contribute the highest revenue across different product categories?

### Key Findings
- Electronics is the only product category with **High (194)** and **Medium (108)** value customer segments.
- Electronics also contains **198** Low-value customers.
- Accessories (434), Fashion (489), Grocery (468), and Home & Kitchen (469) consist entirely of Low-value customer segments based on the defined revenue thresholds.

### Business Insight
Customer spending is highly concentrated in the Electronics category, making it the primary source of both High- and Medium-value customers. In contrast, the remaining product categories are dominated by Low-value customers, indicating lower average customer spending. This suggests that NovaCart should prioritize retaining high-value Electronics customers while exploring cross-selling, upselling, and targeted marketing strategies to increase customer spending in Fashion, Home & Kitchen, Accessories, and Grocery.

## Customer Insight 2

### Business Question
Which customers make repeat purchases within a 90-day period?

### Key Findings
- A total of **2,370 repeat purchases** were recorded within 90 days, compared to **1,102 non-repeat purchases**.
- Repeat purchasing activity significantly exceeds non-repeat purchasing, indicating strong customer retention.
- **Gautami Dara** is the most loyal customer with **22 repeat purchases**, while several other customers completed **12–13 repeat purchases** within the analysis period.

### Business Insight
NovaCart demonstrates strong customer loyalty, with repeat purchases occurring more frequently than non-repeat purchases within the 90-day retention window. This indicates that a substantial portion of customers continue to engage with the platform after their initial purchase. Identifying and rewarding highly loyal customers through personalized offers, loyalty programs, and exclusive promotions can further strengthen customer retention while encouraging occasional buyers to become repeat customers.

## Customer Insight 3

### Business Question
Which regions generate the highest customer demand and revenue?

### Key Findings
- Maharashtra generated the highest revenue (**₹18,78,928.73**) with **712 successful orders**.
- Andhra Pradesh ranked second with **₹17,49,977.37** from **694 orders**.
- Delhi and Gujarat also contributed significantly, generating **₹13.4L** and **₹11.4L** respectively.
- West Bengal recorded the lowest revenue (**₹5,29,449.41**) and the fewest successful orders (**200**) among the analyzed regions.

### Business Insight
Customer demand and revenue are concentrated in a few key states, with Maharashtra and Andhra Pradesh emerging as NovaCart's strongest markets. These regions represent high-value opportunities for continued investment through localized marketing campaigns, inventory optimization, and customer loyalty initiatives. Conversely, lower-performing states such as West Bengal present opportunities to expand market penetration through targeted promotions, regional advertising, and product assortment strategies.

## Customer Insight 4

### Business Question
Which customers consistently spend above the Average Order Value (AOV)?

### Key Findings
- Out of **3,972** successful orders, **1,669** (42.0%) were placed above the Average Order Value, while **2,303** (58.0%) were below the average.
- Several customers consistently placed high-value orders, with **Falguni Peri** and **Triveni Deshpande** leading the list with **10** above-AOV purchases each.
- Other notable high-value customers include **Chakradhar Narula**, **Gautami Dara**, **Chatura Shetty**, and **Gopal Singh**, each recording **9** above-average purchases.

### Business Insight
Approximately **42%** of successful orders exceeded the Average Order Value, indicating a strong segment of customers willing to spend more than the typical order amount. These high-spending customers represent valuable assets for NovaCart and should be targeted through premium product recommendations, exclusive loyalty benefits, early access to new products, and personalized marketing campaigns. Encouraging more customers to transition into this high-value segment can significantly increase overall revenue without relying solely on customer acquisition.

## Customer Insight 5

### Business Question
Which product categories receive the least interest from different customer segments?

### Key Findings
- Accessories is the least purchased product category across all customer segments.
- High-value customers purchased **1,735** units of Accessories, the lowest among all categories within their segment.
- Medium-value customers purchased **600** units of Accessories.
- Low-value customers purchased **559** units of Accessories, making it the least preferred category for every segment.

### Business Insight
Accessories consistently receive the lowest customer demand regardless of spending segment, indicating that the category has limited appeal across NovaCart's customer base. This presents an opportunity to improve category performance through product bundling with popular items, targeted promotional campaigns, personalized recommendations, and a review of the existing product assortment. Increasing customer engagement with Accessories could improve cross-selling opportunities and contribute additional revenue.
