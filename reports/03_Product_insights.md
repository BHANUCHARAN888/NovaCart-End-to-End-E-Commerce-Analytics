## Product Insight 1

### Business Question
Which products contribute the highest percentage of the company's total revenue?

### Key Findings
- **XPS 13** is the highest revenue-generating product, contributing **₹13,44,251.52 (12.63%)** of total company revenue.
- **MacBook Air M3 (12.17%)**, **Victus Gaming Laptop (10.31%)**, and **Inspiron 15 (9.63%)** are the next highest revenue contributors.
- The top five products collectively contribute **53.26%** of the company's total revenue.
- Several products, particularly grocery items such as **Salt 1kg (0.03%)**, **Sunflower Oil 1L (0.04%)**, and **Tea 1kg (0.08%)**, contribute only a very small share of total revenue.

### Business Insight
NovaCart's revenue is highly concentrated among a small number of premium products, particularly laptops and smartphones. The top five products alone generate more than half of the company's total revenue, indicating a strong dependence on high-value electronics. While these products should remain a priority for inventory planning and promotional campaigns, management should also evaluate low-contributing products to determine whether they require improved marketing, repositioning, bundling strategies, or possible discontinuation to optimize the overall product portfolio.

## Product Insight 2

### Business Question
Which products consistently have the lowest sales volume?

### Key Findings
- **Mixer Grinder** recorded the lowest sales volume with **843 units sold**.
- Other low-volume products include **WH-1000XM5 Headphones (857 units)**, **Pavilion 15 (882 units)**, **Hoodie (886 units)**, and **Casual Shoes (891 units)**.
- The difference in sales volume among the lowest-performing products is relatively small, indicating that several products experience similarly low customer demand.

### Business Insight
Several products generate comparatively low sales volume, suggesting limited customer demand or lower market visibility. Products such as Mixer Grinder, WH-1000XM5 Headphones, Pavilion 15, Hoodie, and Casual Shoes should be evaluated for pricing, promotional effectiveness, product positioning, and inventory levels. Management can also consider targeted marketing campaigns, product bundling, or portfolio optimization to improve their sales performance.

## Product Insight 3

### Business Question
Which products demonstrate consistent seasonal demand patterns?

### Key Findings
- **XPS 13** appeared among the top-performing products in **17 months**, making it the most consistently high-demand product.
- **MacBook Air M3** followed closely with **16 peak months**, while **Victus Gaming Laptop** ranked in the top-performing products during **15 months**.
- **Inspiron 15** recorded **9 peak months**, whereas products such as **Galaxy Tab S9** reached the top-performing list only once.
- Premium laptop models consistently dominated monthly revenue across the analysis period.

### Business Insight
NovaCart's seasonal demand is heavily driven by premium laptop products, particularly **XPS 13**, **MacBook Air M3**, and **Victus Gaming Laptop**. Their consistent appearance among the top-performing products indicates stable customer demand throughout the year rather than isolated seasonal spikes. These products should be prioritized for inventory planning, supplier management, and promotional campaigns. Products with limited peak-month appearances may require targeted marketing initiatives or portfolio evaluation to improve their long-term performance.

## Product Insight 4

### Business Question
Which products are most frequently purchased together?

### Key Findings
- **Galaxy Buds 3** and **Pressure Cooker** were the most frequently purchased together, appearing in **59** orders.
- **Galaxy S24** was commonly purchased with **Basmati Rice 5kg (51 orders)** and **Casual Shoes (50 orders)**.
- Other popular product combinations include:
  - AirPods Pro & Victus Gaming Laptop (**48 orders**)
  - Victus Gaming Laptop & Track Pants (**48 orders**)
  - Inspiron 15 & Stone Speaker (**47 orders**)
  - MacBook Air M3 & Tea 1kg (**47 orders**)

### Business Insight
Customer purchasing behavior reveals several frequently bought-together product combinations, indicating opportunities for cross-selling and product bundling. Recommending these complementary products during checkout, creating bundle offers, or offering targeted discounts can increase Average Order Value (AOV) while improving the overall customer shopping experience. Regularly monitoring product pairing trends can also support merchandising and promotional planning.

## Product Insight 5

### Business Question
Which products generate high revenue despite low sales volume, and which products generate high sales volume but low revenue?

### Key Findings
- **Premium Products** identified in the analysis include **XPS 13**, **iPhone 16**, **Galaxy Tab S9**, **Pavilion 15**, and **WH-1000XM5 Headphones**. These products generate above-average revenue despite selling fewer units.
- **High Volume Products** such as **Galaxy Buds 3**, **Pressure Cooker**, **Laptop Bag**, **AirPods Pro**, **Sneakers**, **Basmati Rice 5kg**, and **Salt 1kg** sell above-average quantities but contribute below-average revenue.
- The remaining products fall into the **Normal Product** category, exhibiting balanced sales volume and revenue performance.

### Business Insight
The analysis reveals two distinct product strategies within NovaCart's portfolio. Premium products contribute significant revenue through higher selling prices rather than high sales volume, making them ideal candidates for premium marketing, inventory prioritization, and customer retention initiatives. In contrast, high-volume products generate consistent customer demand but lower revenue per unit, making them suitable for bundle offers, cross-selling campaigns, and volume-based promotional strategies. Managing these two product groups differently can help maximize both profitability and customer engagement.

## Product Insight 6

### Business Question
Which products show consistent growth or decline in sales over time?

### Key Findings
- **Pavilion 15** demonstrated the strongest positive sales trend with **15 growing months** compared to **8 declining months**.
- **Hoodie**, **Airdopes 311**, **Galaxy Buds 3**, and **Galaxy Tab S9** also showed relatively strong growth patterns with more months of revenue increase than decline.
- **Galaxy S24** showed the weakest performance trend, with only **8 growing months** and **15 declining months**, indicating a downward sales pattern.
- Products including **Pressure Cooker**, **Stone Speaker**, **Basmati Rice 5kg**, and **Sunflower Oil 1L** experienced more declining months than growing months.

### Business Insight
Product performance trends indicate that some products are gaining customer demand while others are losing momentum. Growing products such as Pavilion 15, Galaxy Buds 3, and Galaxy Tab S9 should receive priority in inventory planning and marketing investment. Declining products should be analyzed further for possible causes such as changing customer preferences, pricing issues, competition, or reduced promotional visibility. Management can use these trends to optimize product strategy and improve revenue performance.

## Product Insight 7

### Business Question
Which products have the highest average order quantity per purchase?

### Key Findings
- **Sunflower Oil 1L** recorded the highest average order quantity at **2.63 units per order**.
- **Sneakers** ranked second with an average of **2.61 units per order**.
- **MacBook Air M3**, **PlayStation 5**, **Galaxy Buds 3**, and **Sports T-Shirt** each achieved an average order quantity of approximately **2.58 units per order**.
- The average order quantity across products ranged from **2.40 to 2.63 units**, indicating relatively consistent purchasing behavior across the product portfolio.

### Business Insight
Products such as **Sunflower Oil 1L** and **Sneakers** are typically purchased in larger quantities per transaction, making them well-suited for quantity-based promotions, bulk purchase discounts, and inventory optimization strategies. Meanwhile, premium electronics generally maintain lower average quantities per order, reflecting typical customer purchasing behavior for high-value products. Understanding these purchasing patterns enables NovaCart to design targeted pricing strategies, improve inventory planning, and maximize Average Order Value through product bundling and promotional offers.
