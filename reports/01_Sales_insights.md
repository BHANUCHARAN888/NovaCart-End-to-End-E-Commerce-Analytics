## Sales Insight 1

### Business Question
How does monthly revenue change throughout the year?

### Key Findings
- Monthly revenue fluctuates throughout the analysis period rather than following a consistent growth trend.
- September 2024 generated the highest monthly revenue (₹5,43,139.94).
- February 2025 recorded the lowest monthly revenue (₹2,96,727.17).
- Multiple months exceeded ₹500,000 in revenue, indicating several peak sales periods.

### Business Insight
NovaCart's monthly revenue demonstrates noticeable fluctuations across the two-year period, indicating that sales are influenced by seasonal demand, promotional campaigns, or changing customer purchasing behavior rather than continuous growth. The recurring high-revenue months present opportunities to maximize inventory availability and marketing efforts, while lower-performing months should be investigated to identify potential causes such as reduced customer demand or fewer promotional activities.

## Sales Insight 3

### Business Question
Which product categories contribute the most to overall revenue?

### Key Findings
- Electronics is the highest revenue-generating category, contributing **₹95,07,964.37** in sales.
- Fashion and Home & Kitchen generate moderate revenue of **₹4,73,546.54** and **₹4,63,493.17**, respectively.
- Accessories contributes **₹1,62,850.24**.
- Grocery is the lowest-performing category with only **₹39,384.18** in revenue.

### Business Insight
NovaCart's revenue is heavily concentrated in the Electronics category, making it the primary driver of business performance. While this demonstrates strong customer demand for electronics, it also indicates a high dependency on a single product category. The comparatively low contribution from Fashion, Home & Kitchen, Accessories, and Grocery suggests opportunities to improve category performance through targeted marketing campaigns, expanded product offerings, or promotional strategies to diversify revenue sources.

## Sales Insight 5

### Business Question
What is the Average Order Value (AOV) across different months?

### Key Findings
- The Average Order Value remained relatively stable throughout the analysis period, generally ranging between **₹2,400 and ₹2,900**.
- September 2024 recorded the highest AOV at **₹3,213.85**.
- February 2025 recorded the lowest AOV at **₹2,197.98**.
- Several months, including December 2024 and June–July 2025, also achieved above-average order values.

### Business Insight
Customer spending per order remained consistently healthy across most months, indicating stable purchasing behavior. Higher Average Order Value during certain months suggests that customers either purchased higher-value products or larger order combinations. Maintaining these high-AOV periods through effective product bundling, premium product promotions, or personalized recommendations can help increase overall revenue without necessarily increasing the number of orders.

## Sales Insight 7

### Business Question
How does monthly revenue compare year over year (2024 vs. 2025)?

### Key Findings
- Revenue increased in **March, April, May, June, July, October, and November** when compared with the same months in 2024.
- Revenue declined in **January, February, August, September, and December** compared to the previous year.
- The strongest year-over-year improvements occurred during the middle of the year, while the beginning and later months experienced comparatively weaker performance.

### Business Insight
The year-over-year comparison indicates that NovaCart achieved stronger sales performance during the middle months of 2025, suggesting successful growth during this period. However, lower revenue in several months compared to 2024 highlights inconsistent year-over-year performance. Investigating the business activities, promotional campaigns, and customer demand during both high-growth and declining months can help identify the factors influencing these changes and support more effective sales planning in future years.

## Sales Insight 8

### Business Question
Are there any seasonal sales patterns in customer purchasing behavior?

### Key Findings
- September generated the highest cumulative revenue (**₹10,23,983.71**), making it the strongest seasonal sales month.
- February recorded the lowest cumulative revenue (**₹6,38,889.59**).
- January, April, July, November, and December consistently achieved strong sales performance compared to other months.
- Revenue varies noticeably throughout the year, indicating recurring seasonal demand patterns.

### Business Insight
NovaCart exhibits clear seasonal sales patterns, with customer demand peaking during specific months and declining during others. High-performing months such as September, November, and December present opportunities to maximize revenue through inventory readiness and well-timed marketing campaigns. Conversely, lower-performing months like February may require targeted promotions and customer engagement initiatives to maintain a more balanced sales cycle throughout the year.

## Sales Summary

- Revenue demonstrates clear monthly and seasonal fluctuations rather than a consistent growth trend.
- September is the strongest sales month, while February consistently underperforms.
- Electronics is the primary revenue-generating category and contributes the majority of total sales.
- Average Order Value remains relatively stable, indicating consistent customer spending behavior.
- Year-over-year analysis shows stronger performance during the middle months of 2025, although some months experienced lower revenue compared to 2024.
