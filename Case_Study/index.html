01
SALES PERFORMANCE

Understanding revenue performance over time.

BUSINESS QUESTION

How does monthly revenue change throughout the year?

ANALYTICAL APPROACH

Measure monthly revenue from successful transactions to identify changes in sales performance over time.

JOIN DATE_FORMAT GROUP BY SUM()
01_sales_analysis.sql MySQL 

-- Replace with your exact Q1 query

SELECT
    DATE_FORMAT(o.order_date, '%Y-%m') AS month,
    SUM(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price) AS revenue
FROM orders o
JOIN order_items oi
    ON o.order_id = oi.order_id
JOIN payments p
    ON o.order_id = p.order_id
WHERE p.payment_status = 'Success'
GROUP BY month
ORDER BY month;
KEY RESULT September 2024 recorded the highest monthly revenue at ₹543,139.94.

02
CUSTOMER BEHAVIOR

Identifying customer value and retention behavior.

BUSINESS QUESTION

Which customer segments contribute the highest revenue across product categories?

ANALYTICAL APPROACH

Calculate customer revenue and classify customers into revenue-based segments for category-level comparison.

CTE CASE GROUP BY
02_customer_analysis.sql MySQL 

-- Replace with your exact Customer Q1 query

WITH customer_revenue AS (

    SELECT
        c.customer_id,
        c.customer_name,
        SUM(oi.quantity * oi.unit_price) AS revenue

    FROM customers c

    JOIN orders o
        ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id

    JOIN order_items oi
        ON o.order_id = oi.order_id

    GROUP BY
        c.customer_id,
        c.customer_name
)

SELECT
    customer_name,
    revenue,
    CASE
        WHEN revenue >= 20000
            THEN 'High'
        WHEN revenue >= 15000
            THEN 'Medium'
        ELSE 'Low'
    END AS segment
FROM customer_revenue;
BUSINESS QUESTION

Which customers make repeat purchases within a 90-day period?

ANALYTICAL APPROACH

Compare each customer's purchase date with their previous purchase to identify repeat behavior within 90 days.

CTE LAG() DATEDIFF() CASE
02_customer_analysis.sql MySQL 

-- Replace with your exact Customer Q2 query

WITH purchase_history AS (

    SELECT
        o.customer_id,
        o.order_date,

        LAG(o.order_date)
        OVER (
            PARTITION BY o.customer_id
            ORDER BY o.order_date
        ) AS previous_purchase

    FROM orders o
)

SELECT
    customer_id,
    order_date,
    previous_purchase,

    DATEDIFF(
        order_date,
        previous_purchase
    ) AS days_since_previous

FROM purchase_history

WHERE
    previous_purchase IS NOT NULL
    AND
    DATEDIFF(
        order_date,
        previous_purchase
    ) <= 90;
03
PRODUCT PERFORMANCE

Separating product demand from product value.

BUSINESS QUESTION

Which products generate high revenue despite low sales volume?

ANALYTICAL APPROACH

Compare product revenue and sales volume against their respective averages to classify product performance.

CTE AVG() CASE CROSS JOIN
03_product_analysis.sql MySQL 

-- Replace with your exact Product Q5 query

WITH product_metrics AS (

    SELECT
        pr.product_name,

        SUM(
            oi.quantity * oi.unit_price
        ) AS revenue,

        SUM(
            oi.quantity
        ) AS units_sold

    FROM products pr

    JOIN order_items oi
        ON pr.product_id = oi.product_id

    GROUP BY
        pr.product_id,
        pr.product_name
),

averages AS (

    SELECT
        AVG(revenue) AS avg_revenue,
        AVG(units_sold) AS avg_units
    FROM product_metrics
)

SELECT
    pm.*,

    CASE
        WHEN revenue > avg_revenue
             AND units_sold < avg_units
            THEN 'Premium Product'

        WHEN units_sold > avg_units
             AND revenue < avg_revenue
            THEN 'High Volume Product'

        ELSE 'Normal'
    END AS product_type

FROM product_metrics pm
CROSS JOIN averages;
BUSINESS QUESTION

Which products show consistent growth or decline in sales over time?

ANALYTICAL APPROACH

Compare monthly product revenue with the previous period and classify the resulting trend.

CTE LAG() GROWTH RATE CASE
03_product_analysis.sql MySQL 

-- Replace with your exact Product Q6 query

WITH monthly_sales AS (

    SELECT
        pr.product_id,
        pr.product_name,

        DATE_FORMAT(
            o.order_date,
            '%Y-%m'
        ) AS month,

        SUM(
            oi.quantity * oi.unit_price
        ) AS revenue

    FROM products pr
    JOIN order_items oi
        ON pr.product_id = oi.product_id
    JOIN orders o
        ON oi.order_id = o.order_id

    GROUP BY
        pr.product_id,
        pr.product_name,
        month
),

trend AS (

    SELECT
        *,
        LAG(revenue)
        OVER (
            PARTITION BY product_id
            ORDER BY month
        ) AS previous_revenue

    FROM monthly_sales
)

SELECT
    *,
    ROUND(
        (revenue - previous_revenue)
        / previous_revenue * 100,
        2
    ) AS growth_rate

FROM trend;
04
PAYMENT BEHAVIOR

Understanding regional payment preferences.

BUSINESS QUESTION

How do customer payment preferences vary across regions?

ANALYTICAL APPROACH

Rank payment methods independently within each customer state to identify the dominant regional preference.

CTE RANK() PARTITION BY GROUP BY
04_payment_analysis.sql MySQL 

-- Replace with your exact Payment Q4 query

WITH payment_counts AS (

    SELECT
        c.state,
        p.payment_method,
        COUNT(*) AS payment_count

    FROM customers c

    JOIN orders o
        ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id

    JOIN payments p
        ON o.order_id = p.order_id

    GROUP BY
        c.state,
        p.payment_method
)

SELECT
    state,
    payment_method,
    payment_count,

    RANK() OVER (
        PARTITION BY state
        ORDER BY payment_count DESC
    ) AS preference_rank

FROM payment_counts;

08 / RECOMMENDATIONS

What the business should do next.

The analysis points toward practical actions across category strategy, customer growth, product planning and payments.

01 CATEGORY STRATEGY

Protect the Electronics advantage.

Electronics contributes the majority of revenue. Continue investing in high-performing products while gradually expanding other categories to reduce revenue concentration.

ACTION Scale winners + diversify category revenue
02 DEMAND PLANNING

Plan campaigns around demand patterns.

Revenue varies considerably across periods. Use historical peaks and weaker periods to time promotions, campaigns and inventory planning more effectively.

ACTION Align campaigns with seasonal demand
03 PRODUCT STRATEGY

Separate volume strategy from value strategy.

Products with high unit volume are not always the strongest revenue contributors. Evaluate products using both sales volume and revenue before deciding where to promote or invest.

ACTION Prioritize products by value + demand
04 CUSTOMER GROWTH

Turn repeat-purchase behavior into retention strategy.

Identify returning customers and use their purchasing patterns to develop targeted offers, cross-selling opportunities and retention campaigns.

ACTION Build targeted retention campaigns
05 PAYMENTS

Keep digital payments frictionless.

UPI is the strongest payment channel in the analyzed data. Maintaining a smooth digital payment experience can support conversion and customer convenience.

ACTION Optimize the strongest payment channel
